# Eastbridge Trade Finance Risk & Margin Leakage Intelligence

A Business Analyst portfolio project designed to diagnose trade-finance
risk, cash-flow pressure, and margin leakage in a fictional B2B
import-export trading company.

## Project Objective

The project analyzes trade deals from order to payment to understand:

- Expected vs realized profit
- Payment and working-capital risk
- Foreign exchange exposure
- Operational leakage
- Trade-level financial exposure
- Pre-sign trade risk
